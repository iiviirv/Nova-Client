Nova is an open-source client for reaching the open internet on restricted
networks. Bring your own configuration or subscription and connect through
infrastructure you control.

FEATURES
* Broad protocol support via bundled sing-box and Xray cores: VLESS (incl.
  Reality and xhttp/SplitHTTP), VMess, Trojan, Shadowsocks (incl. 2022),
  Hysteria2, TUIC, AmneziaWG and WireGuard, SOCKS and HTTP.
* Nova Radar: an on-device clean-IP scanner that pulls candidate Cloudflare
  ranges, verifies each over TCP and TLS, ranks them by real latency, and
  applies the fastest one in a tap.
* Deploy your own: sign in to your own Cloudflare account and set up your own
  Worker from inside the app - your keys, your edge.
* SNI-block bypass for networks that block the worker domains themselves.
* Per-server live ping through the tunnel, honest reachability verdicts, a full
  core log, and per-network tuning (TLS fingerprint, fragmentation).
* Import from a link, a subscription URL, a QR code, or an AmneziaWG .conf.

Nova bundles and sells nothing. Its networking layer is built on widely used
open-source components, with original scanning, deployment, and interface on
top. The app is licensed under GPL-3.0 (it builds on sing-box); the source is at github.com/iiviirv/Nova-Client.
